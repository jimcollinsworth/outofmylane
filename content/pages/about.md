I'm recently retired after 50 years of working with software and clients. 20 years at Arthur Andersen, then as a guitarist, back to software and a couple startups, and finally finishing up with a data science job after the pandemic. Now I get to sit back and enjoy family, continue thinking, creating, learning, travel, exercise, the Chicago lakefront and AI.

## Curiosity & Mindset

*\"How does that work?\"* is the question that is always in my mind, and comes up for everything: software, hardware, electricity, biology, society, law, music, art, psychology... I'll be the first to admit something I don't know, but at the same time will be fleshing out the alternatives, questioning everything, devising an educated guess and following up as soon as I can to understand.

## \"Out of My Lane\"

The name of this site reflects my habit of venturing into domains well outside my core training.
